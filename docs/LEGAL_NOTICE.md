## 🇪🇸 Versión en Español

### 1. Naturaleza y Carácter del Proyecto
**MintMox** es una aplicación de software independiente, amateur y de código abierto concebida para la gestión, catalogación y seguimiento de colecciones personales del juego de cartas coleccionables *Magic: The Gathering* (MTG).

La aplicación opera bajo una arquitectura **local-first**: toda la información registrada por el usuario reside exclusivamente en la memoria local de su dispositivo mediante una base de datos SQLite integrada. La aplicación base es y será siempre de acceso libre y gratuito.

## 🇬🇧 English Version

### 1. Nature of the Project
**MintMox** is an independent, amateur, open-source software application built for managing, cataloging, and tracking personal collections of *Magic: The Gathering* (MTG) trading cards.

The application operates under a **local-first** architecture: all user portfolio records reside exclusively in the local storage of the user's device via an embedded SQLite database. The core application is and will always remain free of charge.

---

MintMox strictly adheres to and complies with the **Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy**.

> **Official Fan Content Notice:**

In compliance with this policy:
1. **Free Access:** Access to fan content, card images, names, card types, expansions, and game mechanics within MintMox is and will remain 100% free. No payment, paywall, subscription, or mandatory account is required to view cards or manage a local inventory.

### 3. Delimitation of Future Commercial Services & Business Model
The creator explicitly states that **MintMox does not and will never commercialize the intellectual property, trademarks, card artwork, or proprietary assets of Wizards of the Coast or third parties**.

Should paid or subscription features be introduced in the future, they will strictly cover technical infrastructure costs:

1. **Cloud Synchronization & Remote Server Storage (SaaS Infrastructure):**  
   Fees associated with operating remote servers, databases, bandwidth, and end-to-end synchronization protocols enabling users to access their personal collections seamlessly across multiple devices. The user pays for technical hosting and server upkeep, never for MTG card content.
2. **AI Computational Power & Market Model Inference (AI Compute):**  
   Fees associated with raw compute resources, hardware tokens, and algorithm processing needed to run predictive statistical models and market analytics. The user covers the cost of computing capacity, not MTG intellectual property.

The core local management app and card database browser will remain completely free and open.
